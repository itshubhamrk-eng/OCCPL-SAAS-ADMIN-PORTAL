# ADR-0009: Billing — gateway-created invoices, payment state from signed webhooks

- Status: Accepted · Date: 2026-10-14

## Context
D-3/D-4 chose Razorpay and invoices generated by the gateway. Core must turn payments into
subscription periods without double-charging, double-renewing or trusting the browser, and must
run on several instances without a broker (ADR-0004).

## Decision
1. **Razorpay Invoices API behind a `PaymentGateway` port.** Razorpay numbers, emails and hosts the
   invoice; Core keeps the reference, amount, period and state. No Checkout/Orders integration and no
   payment page in the portal: companies pay on Razorpay's hosted page. The port keeps a second
   gateway possible without touching the subscription module.
2. **One invoice per subscription period, enforced by the database.** The invoice row is committed
   as CREATING before the gateway call (HTTP outside transactions, as in ADR-0008), and a generated
   `open_key` with a unique key admits only one CREATING/ISSUED/PARTIALLY_PAID/PAID invoice per
   subscription and period start. Concurrent job runs or a staff click during the job cannot issue two.
3. **Payment state only from signed webhooks or Core's own fetch.** Webhooks are verified on the raw
   body (HMAC-SHA256) and deduplicated by event id; staff can re-fetch an invoice to recover a missed
   webhook. Applying a payment is idempotent on the period: `SubscriptionBilling.applyPayment` renews
   only if the subscription's current period ends where the invoice's period starts.
4. **Lifecycle stays in the subscription module.** Unpaid invoices do not change the subscription;
   the D-5 timers (PAST_DUE → grace → SUSPENDED) do. A paid invoice for a suspended subscription
   reactivates it; for an ended one it is recorded as NOT_APPLICABLE for a manual refund.

## Consequences
+ No card data in Core; small integration surface; safe on several instances and with webhook retries.
+ Manual renewal by staff and payments coexist (ALREADY_COVERED instead of a second renewal).
− Depends on Razorpay emailing invoices and on the webhook being reachable; a missed webhook needs
  the "check payment" action (an automatic reconciliation job may be added if needed).
− Tax (GST) handling is not yet decided (D-11); refunds/credit notes are out of scope (B-7).
